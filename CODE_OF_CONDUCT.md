# Code of Conduct

Orbit contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
